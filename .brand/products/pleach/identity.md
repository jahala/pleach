## Positioning

The deterministic conductor between **tend** (the feature ledger that decides what “done” means) and **umbel** (the boundary that runs one unit of agent work). pleach takes a plan — a DAG of nodes — isolates each node in its own detached git worktree, enforces gates (conflict-marker scan · smoke · cross-provider audit), and publishes a `node/<id>` branch only for work that mechanically passed them — so a swarm of stochastic agents can build unattended without garbage reaching the trunk.

## Mark

Two living branches *pleached* — trained and woven over-and-under — into one upright trunk: the **plum** strand (`#97539B`, the verified weave) and the **leaf-green** strand (`#4A9E3F`, the work being trained in). The weave says the name (pleaching: interleaving living branches into a single structure) and the bet — many agents’ branches, interwoven into one trunk only where they are verified. The lowercase wordmark `pleach` sits in ink beside it; the mark carries the colour, the word stays calm.

## Logo usage

- Minimum size: 20px mark height.
- Clear space: half the mark height on every side.
- Fills are exact — plum `#97539B` and leaf `#4A9E3F` on paper; on dark, plum holds and leaf brightens to `#9FD08A`. Never recolour outside these.
- Never: stretch, rotate, add a third strand, outline the wordmark, or set the wordmark in anything but the body face.
